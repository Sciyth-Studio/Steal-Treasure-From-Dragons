## COMPOSITION

This thumbnail competes against a wall of others at postage-stamp size. Shrunk to
the width of a thumb and blurred, the subject and what is happening must read in
one second.

- At most THREE big foreground shapes, each recognisable from its silhouette alone.
- One dominant subject, clearly the largest thing in the frame.
- Separated silhouettes with clear gaps of sky between them; nothing overlaps
  confusingly.
- Light against dark, bold saturated colour, crisp dark outlines.
- Never shoot flat and straight on at eye level; use a low angle or a slight tilt.
- Catch the moment just before or during the peak action, never after.

## STOPPING POWER

COLOUR: one dominant saturated hue with an opposite accent. Push saturation high.
Muted, grey or muddy palettes vanish in a feed.

CONTRAST: the main subject is the brightest thing in its area, with one clean rim
light separating it from the simpler background.

## RESTRAINT

Over-rendering reads as AI, not as Roblox. One main light, ambient sky light and
soft contact shadows. Keep out unless asked: heavy bloom, god rays, haze, depth-
of-field blur, rim light on everything, air full of particles, lens flares,
vignettes. The effects the request names are the only effects. Restraint applies
to effects, never to modelling detail.

Never invent characters, creatures or props the request did not ask for.
Do not output an explanation, a written prompt or a description. Generate the
finished thumbnail image.
