## CORE RULES

### 1. ROBLOX DESIGN LANGUAGE

The world is a finished, professionally rendered Roblox game scene.

NOT LOW POLY: this is a high-detail, high-polygon render, like the top Roblox
Discover icons. Every edge is softly bevelled and catches a highlight, curved
shapes are perfectly smooth, and lighting falls off in smooth gradients across
each form. Never low-poly, never faceted, never flat-shaded, never PS1-style or
papercraft. Blocky shapes are allowed only where Roblox itself is blocky (the
avatar's head, torso and limbs, and the BLOCKY DRAGON, which is built from chunky
rectangular blocks and wedges as on its sheet), and even those have bevelled edges.

SURFACES: solid colour on smooth matte or lightly glossy plastic, with no texture
noise: no repeating raised circles, no rows of small bumps, no grain, no seams.
Gold coins, gems and treasure are smooth, glossy and clean.

THE TWO EXCEPTIONS are the ground and the dragon. The ground may be a classic Roblox baseplate with a
regular grid of small, shallow, rounded-square Roblox studs (NOT round LEGO studs).
The dragon carries the small raised studs shown on its sheet, all over its body
and wings. Studs appear nowhere else: never on the character, the treasure or
any other object.

Exactly square, 1:1, 1024 x 1024 pixels, PNG. The artwork fills the entire

## ICON COMPOSITION

An icon is seen tiny, in a grid of other icons. It has to read as one bold shape.

- Three subjects at most: the hero, the item he carries and the dragon. Nothing else.
- Crop close and let the subjects run off the edges.
- Build the composition on a diagonal. A centred, upright, symmetrical pose is
  static.
- Background is one simple cue: by default a flat green studded baseplate
  meeting a plain bright blue sky at a low horizon, like a fresh Roblox
  baseplate. No scenery, no props, no crowds.

## STOPPING POWER

COLOUR: one dominant saturated hue with its opposite as the accent. Push
saturation high. Muted, grey or muddy palettes vanish in a grid.

CONTRAST: the subject is the brightest thing in the square, with one clean rim
light separating it from a simpler background.

ANGLE: never flat and straight on at eye level. Use a low angle or a slight tilt.

MOMENT: the instant just before or during the peak action, never after it.

## RESTRAINT

Over-rendering reads as AI, not as Roblox. One main light, ambient sky light and
soft contact shadows. Keep out unless asked: heavy bloom, god rays, haze, depth-
of-field blur, rim light on everything, air full of particles, lens flares,
vignettes. The effects the request names are the only effects. Restraint applies
to effects, never to modelling detail.

Never invent characters, creatures or props the request did not ask for.
Do not output an explanation, a written prompt or a description. Generate the
finished icon image.
