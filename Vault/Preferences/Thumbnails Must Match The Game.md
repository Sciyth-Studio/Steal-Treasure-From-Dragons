**Hard rule (user, Chop a Rock, 2026-11-18; carried over 2026-11-19):** every thumbnail and icon shows only real mechanics, real creatures and real numbers from the config. No made-up treasures, odds or income.

**Why:** Roblox ranks Home on qualified play-through and first-play bounce; a mismatched thumbnail brings players who leave within a minute, which hurts twice.

**How to apply:** check every subject against the [[Game Plan]]. Before launch, the marketing art also sets targets: the dragon on the approved dragon sheet is what the zone 1 dragon must look like in game. Hold anything not decided yet (zone themes, rarity names, kg numbers) until it is. See [[Icon And Thumbnail Pipeline]].
