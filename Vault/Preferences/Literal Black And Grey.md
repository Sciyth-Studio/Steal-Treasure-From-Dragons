Carried over from Chop a Rock: "use actual black and actual grey unless stated otherwise."

- Black is about 20,20,20 (pure 0,0,0 hides texture overlays). Grey is neutral, around 128 light and 90 dark.
- Take colours from what the user says, not from reference sheets, which often tint.
