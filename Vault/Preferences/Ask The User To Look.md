Carried over from Chop a Rock: "if you need to look at something just ask me."

- For motion, timing or whether something shows at all, take one or two stills, then ask the user to watch it.
- Also ask when it's easier to see than to probe: a GUI `screen_capture` can't show, a click the test mouse can't reach.
